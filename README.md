Exactly one word describing the entrant's boss: no phrase, no caption, no explanation. A judge model compares the words two at a time on how funny the word is, how true it rings, and how fresh it feels.

## The word

> Bobblehead

One word, 10 characters: the boss as a dashboard doll — a wobbling head on a suit that nods at everything the boss's boss says, then passes it down as your urgent priority. Agrees with whoever spoke last, in every room, every time. The nodding is the whole job description.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (10 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-word, 1–30-character gate.
